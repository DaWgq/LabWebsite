实验室简介

复杂系统与多智能体实验室 (CSMALab)

复杂系统与多智能体实验室(CSMALab)面向未来智能系统中的群体智能和复杂决策问题，开展多智能体强化学习、协同控制、复杂网络建模、自动驾驶与智能交通、自动化仿真与复杂电路设计等研究。实验室依托高性能计算与仿真平台，聚焦从基础理论到工程应用的全链路创新，面向复杂工程系统与多样化应用场景，培养具备系统思维和工程落地能力的复合型人才。

Complex Systems and Multi-Agent Laboratory (CSMA Lab)

The Complex Systems and Multi-Agent Laboratory (CSMA Lab) focuses on emerging intelligence and complex decision-making in interactive environments, covering multi-agent reinforcement learning, cooperative control, complex networks, autonomous driving and intelligent transportation, as well as large-scale simulation and circuit/system design automation. Leveraging advanced computing and simulation platforms, the lab bridges theory and real-world applications in complex engineered systems and cultivates researchers and engineers with strong system-level thinking and practical impact.
